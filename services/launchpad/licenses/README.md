Consult the applicable component terms before redistribution. These notices
do not select a new license or grant additional rights.

## Third-party notices

Retain upstream license and attribution notices required by each dependency.
A dependency manifest or SBOM entry does not establish redistribution permission
or satisfy notice obligations.

The [supply-chain tools](../security/supplychain/README.md) inventory dependency
licenses. `NOASSERTION` records an unknown conclusion, not redistribution approval.
