# D658: The fee denom of each asset is recorded at request time

Block `[decision.658]` of spec.kvx at revision `2a1033aa2`. Generated from the merged specification; edit the fragment, not this file.

## Value

RequestFromProgram records the bank denom of the first accepted fee per asset under web/feedenom; a later fee of another denom for that asset is LXP_ASSET_MISMATCH; the split pays in the recorded denom and refuses an asset without one.

## Basis

splitProgramFee probes balances to choose a denom (modules/xweb/keeper/program_fulfil.go:233-248), so a third-party deposit of another mapped denom changes the outcome.
