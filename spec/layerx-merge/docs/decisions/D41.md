# D41: Subscription lag signalling

Block `[decision.41]` of spec.kvx at revision `2a1033aa2`. Generated from the merged specification; edit the fragment, not this file.

## Value

A lag object carrying the dropped count is the final subscription overflow behaviour; no server-side close is added to the pinned RPC notifier.

## Basis

A receipt-log listener that falls behind keeps its registration: logs it cannot take are dropped and counted in lagged, which is reported ahead of its next log (rpc/filter.go:241-262, :356).
