# D813: Webhook finality on the merged chain is the committed block

Block `[decision.813]` of spec.kvx at revision `2a1033aa2`. Generated from the merged specification; edit the fragment, not this file.

## Value

A delivery is final once the receipt's block is committed; the kernel words checkpoint-finalised and paxeer-finalised are retired after a wave in which block-committed is accepted beside them, so subscribers can move their minimum first.

## Basis

The vocabulary names checkpoint certificates and the Paxeer anchor (services/platform/hosted/webhooks/src/events.rs:24-27), both kernel settlement stages the merged chain does not have.
