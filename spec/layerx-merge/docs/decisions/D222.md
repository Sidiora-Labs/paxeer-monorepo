# D222: escrowPartialCapture and escrowTimeout reuse the capture and release payloads

Block `[decision.222]` of spec.kvx at revision `2a1033aa2`. Generated from the merged specification; edit the fragment, not this file.

## Value

escrowPartialCapture submits ordinal 3 with the 80-byte capture payload and escrowTimeout submits ordinal 5 with the 64-byte release payload; no new payload encoder is added.

## Basis

The kernel escrow decoder routes ordinals 2 and 3 to decode_capture and 4 and 5 to decode_release (src/modules/escrow/lx_escrow_module.c:152-163), and the Go encoders exist at modules/layerxpay/types/payload_escrow.go:11-12.
