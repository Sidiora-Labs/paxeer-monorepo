# D441: Service route registrations at HEAD are the route authority for clients

Block `[decision.441]` of spec.kvx at revision `2a1033aa2`. Generated from the merged specification; edit the fragment, not this file.

## Value

A client operation is served only when a launchpad service registers its method and path at HEAD; operation descriptors and client literals do not establish a route.

## Basis

operationDefinitions (sdk/launchpad/packages/sdk/src/operations.gen.ts:205) was generated from a v2 bundle that is absent at HEAD, and 33 of its 201 operations match no registered route.
