# D1703: spot runs on PECOR at 0x1021-0x1025

Source: `[decision.1703]` in [the installed specification](../../spec.kvx). Programme carry status: **kept**; source carry: `new`.

## Value

Spot trading is the PECOR set at 0x1021 PECOR, 0x1022 Orders, 0x1023 StopOrders, 0x1024 Quoter, 0x1025 Vault; production serves spot.NewServedSet (decision.322, decision.334).

## Basis

`modules/spot/types/keys.go` declares all five PECOR addresses. `v6120StoreUpgrades` in `node/upgrades.go` adds the spot store; `node/upgrades_v6120.go` initializes it through the v6.12.0 plan.

## Carry-forward

The served PECOR set assigns 0x1021 to market/swap execution, 0x1022 to limit orders, 0x1023 to stop orders, 0x1024 to quoting and 0x1025 to the liquidity vault. The spot store is added by the v6.12.0 plan (decisions.322 and .334).
