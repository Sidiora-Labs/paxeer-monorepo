# D204: Bank mirror under a native layerx denom

Block `[decision.204]` of spec.kvx at revision `2a1033aa2`. Generated from the merged specification; edit the fragment, not this file.

## Value

Each kernel asset mirrors into bank as layerx/<64 lowercase hex of the asset id>; register mints the cap, or 2^128-1 when unbounded, to the bank address of the issuance account; every transfer leg becomes a bank SendCoins; the layerxledger module account holds Minter and Burner.

## Basis

Tokenfactory subdenoms are capped at 44 characters (modules/tokenfactory/types/denoms.go:17), too short for a 64-digit asset id, and kernel accounts map to bank addresses through BankAddress (modules/lxphost/accountmap.go:78) with IssuanceAccountID at modules/lxphost/accountmap.go:64.
