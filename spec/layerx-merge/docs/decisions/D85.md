# D85: Giga block fee-token charge destination

Block `[decision.85]` of spec.kvx at revision `2a1033aa2`. Generated from the merged specification; edit the fragment, not this file.

## Value

After a giga block the fee-token charge is asserted at the fee-token holding account, not at the coinbase: EndBlock sweeps each coinbase's fee tokens to the fee collector (modules/evm/keeper/abci.go:165-178) and ProcessBlock routes the collector to the fee-token holding account (modules/evm/keeper/feecollect.go:17), which modules/evm/keeper/abci_test.go:119-121 requires; neither step is removed.

## Basis

modules/evm/keeper/abci.go:165-178 sweeps coinbase fee tokens; the charge reaches the coinbase at node/app.go:2405.
