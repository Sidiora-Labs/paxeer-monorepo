## Value

The native-denom airdrop path (ExecuteAirdrop, ClaimAirdrop, AirdropEntitlement, ClaimAirdropForEpoch), the holding-history writer and bank balance-change hook, the per-market protocol counter and their genesis fields and key builders are removed from the current keeper; prefixes 0x03 and 0x20-0x25 stay reserved and genesis refuses non-empty generation 1 fields. Replay safety rests on CheckNoNativeMarkets: no native market exists at the v6.12.0 migration, so the hook never wrote state and its removal changes no historical result; the v6.10 replay keeper keeps its own copy.
