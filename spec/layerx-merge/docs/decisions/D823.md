# D823: Payment receipt evidence is the PortableReceiptV2 document

Block `[decision.823]` of spec.kvx at revision `2a1033aa2`. Generated from the merged specification; edit the fragment, not this file.

## Value

Receipt evidence is PortableReceiptV2 with format layerx-receipt-proof-v2, verificationLevel receipted or committed, rawTransaction, signedReceipt, receiptDigest and receiptKey; committed evidence adds landingBlockHeight, blockHeader, receiptInclusion, commit and validatorSet in place of kernel batch headers, Merkle proofs and guarantor checkpoints.

## Basis

LayerXReceiptEvidence carries a base64 kernel receipt at services/platform/middleware/seller/src/index.ts:73-78 and PaymentCommitmentEvidence carries kernel batch and checkpoint material at services/platform/middleware/seller/src/commitment.ts:14-25.
