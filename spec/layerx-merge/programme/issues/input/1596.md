# Issue 1596: [Coherence] The full-commit-header-limit finding cites the wrong line in autobahn.proto for the 100-header cap

## Defect and impact

The record's blocking finding full-commit-header-limit says autobahn.proto:152 caps FullCommitQC headers at 100. In the file, FullCommitQC.headers and its max_count = 100 are on line 141. Line 152 is the closing brace of the TimeoutQC message, whose votes cap sits on line 151. The repair task for this finding will be built from the citation, so the wrong line sends whoever does the repair to the timeout certificate instead of the header limit.

Kind: semantic. Severity: minor.
