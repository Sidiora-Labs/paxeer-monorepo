# Issue 1585: [Coherence] The audit record declares the market contract pages missing, but all three exist under spec/layerx-merge/contracts/

## Defect and impact

The record has a finding, missing-interface-contracts, and three doc entries (doc.13 to doc.15) that mark contracts/markets.md, contracts/perps-data.md and contracts/perps-client.md as missing at the audited revision. Interface-registry contract links are relative to spec/layerx-merge, and all three pages exist there with thousands of lines, including the #hooks section and the useMarketConfig row. The record therefore files a false finding and proposes a repair to 'restore' pages that already exist. The downstream registry-* findings also tell readers to restore contracts/perps-client.md#hooks when that section only needs reconciling.

Kind: cross-system. Severity: major.
