## Defect and impact

The record's registry-contract-pages-missing finding and doc.017–doc.021 say contracts/services.md, clients.md, operations.md, launchpad.md and explorer-frontend-config.md are absent at this revision. Registry contract paths in interfaces.kvx are relative to the specification directory, and every one of these pages exists there. The anchor the finding names for 4.go-security-control-new-runtime, contracts/services.md#launchpad-security, also resolves to a section that documents this exact code root. The finding proposes retargeting interface-registry rows, so acting on it would rewrite correct contract anchors and open a repair task for a defect that does not exist.

Kind: connective. Severity: major.
