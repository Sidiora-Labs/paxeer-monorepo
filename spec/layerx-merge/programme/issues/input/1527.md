# Issue 1527: [Coherence] Resolutions pin phoenix and js-cookie to one version, but the app ships another

## Defect and impact

The commit adds exact resolutions for phoenix 1.6.17 and js-cookie 3.0.7 and records them as floors. The direct-dependency entries use caret ranges, and the lock resolves those ranges separately: phoenix to 1.8.16 and js-cookie to 3.0.8. The explorer front end therefore bundles a Phoenix channel client two minors past the version the commit pins and records. The pinned copies sit in the lock unused by the app.

Kind: connective. Severity: minor.

Reviewed commits: d3a96e6089f927d450d18466d1c1447f22cfa8e6

## Expected versus actual

Expected: The direct dependency and the resolution agree, so the app ships the version the commit pins and checks against the browser channel protocol of the explorer backend.

Actual: Two copies of each package are locked. The app ships phoenix 1.8.16 and js-cookie 3.0.8, while the resolutions and the floors file name 1.6.17 and 3.0.7.
