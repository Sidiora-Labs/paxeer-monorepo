## Defect and impact

The Python discovery repair in [7a2a710bef68175548dcccf2a2de0958688d462b](https://github.com/Sidiora-Labs/paxeer-monorepo/commit/7a2a710bef68175548dcccf2a2de0958688d462b) adds two shipped files without their required SDK pipeline classification:
