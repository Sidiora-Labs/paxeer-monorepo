# Issue 1553: [Coherence] The cumulative-gas and fee-token rulings for 22.23.8 cite decision 2386, which is the batch-header retirement decision

## Defect and impact

Task 22.23.8 do_6 and do_7, along with qualification finding 21.87.1.2, base the TestReceiptFeeFields and TestAccepterReceiptsASendAcrossASeal rulings on decision 2386. Decision 2386 is actually about deleting the retired lxp_batch declarations, and it affects only 1.0.9 and 1.0.10. No decision in the spec records the ruling that cumulative gas is receipt-bound, or the fee-token digest re-pin, so the ruling the rewritten expectations depend on cannot be found and 22.23.8 is missing from every decision's affects list.

Kind: semantic. Severity: minor.
