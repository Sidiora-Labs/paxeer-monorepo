# Issue 1676: [Coherence] The balance boundary wraps the whole overview panel, so every refresh and every balance failure hides unrelated stats and controls

## Defect and impact

PortfolioBalanceBoundary replaces its children whenever either balance source is not "ready". It wraps the entire PortfolioOverviewPanel, not just the balances strip. Each `refresh()` first publishes an all-"loading" snapshot. So pressing the panel's own Refresh button (which bumps `refreshCounter`) unmounts the whole panel and shows a loading notice until both reads finish. A balance error also hides PnL, trade stats and daily PnL, which come from separate hooks and have nothing to do with the failed reads.

Kind: semantic. Severity: minor.
