# Issue 1618: [Coherence] The audit record marks 21 interface contract pages as missing, but every one exists under spec/layerx-merge/contracts/

## Defect and impact

The record rates doc.014 to doc.034 as status "missing", and its finding registry-contract-pages-missing says every contract page this package's registry rows point to is absent. Registry contract keys such as "contracts/sdk-js.md#package" are relative to the specification directory, which is the convention the contracts index states. All 21 pages exist at spec/layerx-merge/contracts/<name>.md, and the cited anchor resolves: sdk-js.md has a "## package" heading. The false finding produces 22 spurious repair and rewrite items and wrongly says the SDK, bridge and explorer interfaces have no contract documentation.

Kind: semantic. Severity: major.
