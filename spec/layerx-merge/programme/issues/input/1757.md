# Issue 1757: [Coherence] Two targets with observed assertion failures are counted as not_run instead of failed, so the header undercounts failures

## Defect and impact

The closure inventory marks layerx-agentd and layerx-sdk as not_run (timeout exit 124), yet each block records a real assertion failure seen before the timeout: agent_rpc_dispatch.rs:2560 and native_effect.rs:495. The task asks that every target failing now be recorded with its observed and assumption sentences, and that a repaired target which fails again be recorded failed with its first failing line. As written, the header reports targets_failed = 4 and targets_not_run = 4, when at least six targets are known to fail. Anyone relying on the header counts will believe two fewer targets need repair.

Kind: semantic. Severity: major.

Expected: layerx-agentd and layerx-sdk are recorded result = "failed" with their first failing line, and the header reads targets_failed = 6 and targets_not_run = 2.
