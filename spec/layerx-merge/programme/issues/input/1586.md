# Issue 1586: [Coherence] The two contract-drift findings point at the wrong lines of services.md and clients.md

## Defect and impact

The audit record says the retired-router wording is on lines 40 and 48 of contracts/services.md and line 19 of contracts/clients.md. Those lines actually hold the platform-agent-boundary row, the platform-indexer row and operation R (receipt read). The text the findings describe is on services.md lines 39 and 46 and clients.md line 20. These two documents have not changed since the audited revision 6d2c32a65a, so the line numbers were wrong when recorded, and any repair task built from this record will be sent to the wrong rows.

Kind: semantic. Severity: minor.
