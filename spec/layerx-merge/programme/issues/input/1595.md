## Defect and impact

The record says contracts/consensus-fork.md and contracts/sequencer.md do not exist and proposes restoring them or repointing the registry rows. Registry contract paths are relative to spec/layerx-merge, and both pages are there. consensus-fork.md even has the #openapi anchor and the row for 15.fmt-consensus-rpc-openapi-j57. Acting on this finding would create a repair task with nothing to repair, and the two docs are marked 'missing' when they were never read.

Kind: semantic. Severity: major.
