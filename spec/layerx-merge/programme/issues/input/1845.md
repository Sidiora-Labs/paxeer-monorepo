# Issue 1845: [Coherence] Task 22.39.151 guards the wrong step: the registry variables are read by the middleware step, and the plan explicitly leaves that step without a condition or the variables

## Defect and impact

Gotcha 3578, decision 2883 and task 22.39.151 all assume that the quickstart benchmark step is the only step of build-lint-test that reads LAYERX_TEN_LINE_REGISTRY_SNAPSHOT or LAYERX_TEN_LINE_REGISTRY_URL. That is wrong. The benchmark step runs `make platform-build-ten-line`, which calls 104.16.5.sh --build-artifacts, and that mode never reads the registry. The step that needs it is 'Build the framework integrations and scan client bundles for secrets': its `make platform-test-middleware` ends in `$(MAKE) platform-test-ten-line`, which runs the verify mode, and verify refuses unless one of the two variables is set. The plan moves both variables into the benchmark step's env, so the middleware step loses them and fails with exit 78 whether or not the repository has the registry. The new test requires that step to stay unconditioned, so the primary job turns red instead of being skipped. Task 22.39.78 attempt 3 now depends on this broken plan and is told to 'change nothing'.

Kind: semantic. Severity: major.

## Expected versus actual

Expected: The presence guard and the variables belong on the step that runs the ten-line verify, which today is the middleware step through platform-test-middleware. Alternatively, move platform-test-ten-line out of platform-test-middleware into its own guarded step. The upload step's condition should follow whichever step writes ten-line-counts.json.

Actual: The spec tells the next attempt to guard and scope the variables to the benchmark build step, which never reads them, and to keep the middleware step unconditioned without them. Once implemented, build-lint-test fails at the middleware step on every push and pull request, including in repositories that have the registry.
