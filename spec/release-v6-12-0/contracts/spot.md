# Spot venue contract

This contract defines the spot venue of Paxeer X Network for the v6.12.0 release: the PECOR interface the Hyperpax application calls, served natively as a precompile set backed by the modules/spot keeper.

## Implementation

The PECOR v2 engine (the semantic source: contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/quoter/MetaAGQuoter.sol) keeps all of its state in plain storage: inventory per token, order records with an id counter, active lists and fee accruals. The v2 vault is a donation inventory that issues no shares (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol). The provider shares, the LP events and the share pointer come from the application ABI (apps/perps/src/core/network/spot_abi/PECORVault.ts, apps/perps/src/core/network/spot_abi/PECORVault.ts), so the share accounting of Vault share accounting is defined by this contract, with its own invariants. Each of these is expressible in keeper state, so the spot venue is the PECOR set at fixed addresses.

- Authority: the application ABI (apps/perps/src/core/network/spot_abi/PECOR.ts, generated by the spot integration kit) fixes every selector, signature, mutability and event topic; the Launch-x meta-ag v2 sources fix the semantics. Source paths identify the declarations and rules; where the ABI names an error differently from the v2 source, the mapping table under Errors names both.
- Served: PECOR at 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000001021, PECOROrders at 0x1022, PECORStopOrders at 0x1023, PECORQuoter at 0x1024, PECORVault at 0x1025.
- Not served: 0x1026 (PECORVaultShare) carries no code, because each vault share is a bank denom spot/share/<token> with its own ERC20 native pointer returned by `shareTokens(address)`; 0x1027 (PECORRouter) carries no code, because the record maps no router behaviour that the five rails and `multicall(bytes[])` do not already cover. Both addresses are gated by the v6.12.0 activation and stay without code.
- Replaced: the relayer price oracle (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/oracle/PriceOracle.sol) is replaced by modules/pricefeed GetPrice; the transaction tracker is replaced by the EVM logs of every state change.
- Changed semantic: resting limit, stop-limit and stop-loss orders execute in EndBlock against the pool index price instead of waiting for an external keeper transaction (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol), with every EndBlock step bounded per block; the execute and batch methods stay callable and apply the same rule inside a transaction.
- Token model: ERC20 tokens (USDL and every bridged ERC20) are ERC20 balances of the spot module EVM address, pulled by transferFrom against an allowance to the rail that pulls and paid by transfer through the EVM keeper; native tokens (uhpx and the SID denom) are bank balances of the spot module account moved by bank send with no allowance (see Token model).
- ABI: every served selector, event topic and error selector equals the value computed from the application ABI (apps/perps/src/core/network/spot_abi/PECOR.ts) extended by the error entries of Added error entries; the application changes its addresses (apps/perps/src/core/spot/addresses.ts) and the items listed under Hyperpax call sequence.

## Token model

Every registered token has a custody kind, recorded in TokenParams.kind: ERC20 or NATIVE. The spot module account (authtypes.NewModuleAddress("spot")) and its EVM address (GetEVMAddressOrDefault, modules/evm/keeper/address.go) hold all custody; book reserves, escrows and fee accruals are keeper records over that one custody balance per token.

| Token | Kind | Custody | Decimals | Token argument | Price source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| USDL (stable) | ERC20 | ERC20 balance of the spot module EVM address | 6 (contracts/libraries/Constants.sol:16), checked against decimals() at registration | the existing USDL contract 0x85FcD13735F4309833A503EE804ea32395851479 (contracts/libraries/Constants.sol:14) | fixed 1e18 |
| PAX | NATIVE | uhpx bank balance of the spot module account, the base denom (modules/evm/keeper/params.go) | 6 from the denom metadata exponent, checked against the pinned 6 | the uhpx native pointer created | pricefeed market 11 |
| SID | NATIVE | bank balance of the SID denom layerxbridgetypes.SidioraDenom() (modules/layerxbridge/types/keys.go) | 6 from the denom metadata exponent, checked against the pinned 6 | the SID native pointer created | pricefeed market 12 |
| Vault share of T | share | bank denom spot/share/<lowercase hex of T>, minted and burned by the spot module account | decimals of T | pointer returned by `shareTokens(address)` | not priced |

USDL has no bank denom and no pointer at its address. A bridged ERC20 registered later by MsgUpdateParams takes kind ERC20 and the decimals its decimals() returns (at most 36, the rule of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol, else `UnsupportedToken()`).

- Resolution: an ERC20 token argument is the contract address recorded in TokenParams; a NATIVE token argument resolves to its denom through GetNativePointee (modules/evm/keeper/pointer.go), and its decimals come from the denom metadata exponent as the pointer precompile reads them (precompiles/pointer/pointer.go).
- Inbound ERC20 in a transaction: the rail that pulls (0x1021 for swaps and market orders, 0x1022 for limit placement, 0x1023 for stop placement, 0x1025 for deposits) makes a nested evm.Call with its own address as msg.sender to `transferFrom(caller, module EVM address, amount)` on the token, the nested-call pattern of precompiles/xweb/xweb.go, so the user's allowance is to that rail address. The credited amount is the balanceOf delta of the module EVM address across the call, the measured receipt of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol: a zero receipt reverts `ZeroAmount()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol), and a receipt that differs from the exact input of swapExactOut reverts `UnsupportedInputTransfer()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol).
- Outbound ERC20 in a transaction: a nested evm.Call with the module EVM address as caller to `transfer(recipient, amount)`. Outside a transaction (EndBlock, the upgrade handler and message handlers) the same transfer runs through CallEVM from the module address (modules/evm/keeper/evm.go), which refuses inside an EVM context and is used only there. Delivery is exact: the module and recipient balance deltas must both equal amount, else `UnsupportedTransfer()`, and a payout to the module EVM address itself reverts `UnsupportedTransfer()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol).
- Custody reads: an ERC20 custody balance is balanceOf of the module EVM address, read by StaticCallEVM (modules/evm/keeper/evm.go) outside a transaction and by a nested static call inside one.
- Native moves: inbound and outbound NATIVE amounts move by bank SendCoins (precompiles/utils/expected_keepers.go) between the bank address of the caller (GetPaxAddressOrDefault, precompiles/utils/expected_keepers.go) and the spot module account; no allowance is read or needed. The spot module account is in allowedReceivingModAcc, so bank sends to it are accepted.
- Allowance: only ERC20 inputs need an allowance, granted to the rail address that pulls; the application keeps its allowance step for USDL (apps/perps/src/hooks/spot/useSpotApprovals.ts, apps/perps/src/hooks/spot/useSpotApprovals.ts) retargeted to that address and skips it for PAX and SID.
- Failure: an ERC20 call that reverts or returns false reverts the whole call with `SafeERC20FailedOperation(address)` carrying the token address; a bank move that fails, as an insufficient balance, reverts `SafeERC20FailedOperation(address)` carrying the native pointer address. A token argument with no registered kind, or a CW20 pointer, reverts `UnsupportedTransfer()`.
- Native value: native PAX and the PAX token are one uhpx balance, so no wrap exists. swapExactInNative and depositNative take the attached value, which the EVM credits to the rail address, and move value / 1e12 uhpx from the rail address into the module account by bank send; a value that is not a whole multiple of 1e12 wei reverts `UnsupportedInputTransfer()`, the exact-input rule of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol. swapExactInToNative and withdrawNative pay uhpx by bank send. `WETH()` returns the uhpx pointer.
- Atomicity: pull, payout, fee accrual and book update land in one cached context written only on success (modules/launchpad/keeper/swap.go shape); a failing ERC20 call or bank move discards the cached context with it. EndBlock settles through the same keeper path, its ERC20 legs through CallEVM.
- Gas: a nested ERC20 call runs on the gas of the outer call and is charged to the caller; CallEVM in EndBlock runs at gas price zero, is charged to no account and is bounded by the EndBlock budgets.
- Native PAX sales use the uhpx balance directly. `buildSwapExactInNative` in `apps/perps/src/core/spot/calls.ts` converts the six-decimal uhpx amount to attached wei by multiplying by 1e12; no wrapped-token deposit is needed.

## Pricing

Prices come from modules/pricefeed/keeper GetPrice(ctx, marketId), which returns a Price structure containing index, mark, timestamp, height, sources and freshness; the spot venue uses the index price at 18 decimals and no other source.

- Stable tokens price at exactly 1e18 without a read.
- A non-stable token reads the market id of its TokenParams; PAX reads 11 and SID reads 12.
- fresh false refuses every trade and fill of that token with `StalePrice()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/oracle/PriceOracle.sol); the quoter returns the stale flag instead of reverting.
- A non-stable token with market id 0 refuses orders with `InvalidOracle()` and swaps with `StalePrice()`.

The pool prices every trade by its index price (the PECOR convert rule, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECORMath.sol):

```
gross    = amountIn * priceIn * 10^decOut / (priceOut * 10^decIn)       rounded down
volume   = amountIn * priceIn / 10^decIn                                  USD, 18 decimals
feeBps   = base + (volume >= 10,000e18 ? tier1 : 0) + (volume >= 100,000e18 ? tier2 : 0)
fee      = gross * feeBps / 10000                                        rounded down
afterFee = gross - fee
impact   = min(volume * scalarBps / reserveUSD(tokenOut), 500)           bps, 0 when disabled
net      = afterFee - afterFee * impact / 10000
retained = gross - net                                                   reserved as the fee accrual of tokenOut
```

On 0x1021 base, tier1, tier2, the impact flag and scalarBps are swapFeeBps, tier1FeeBps, tier2FeeBps, priceImpactEnabled and priceImpactScalarBps; on 0x1022 and 0x1023 they are the order pricing set orderFeeBps, orderTier1FeeBps, orderTier2FeeBps, orderPriceImpactEnabled and orderPriceImpactScalarBps (Fees). reserveUSD is the spendable book of the output token times its index price. Exact-output quotes invert the formula through the tier bands (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol) and round the input up (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/quoter/MetaAGQuoter.sol). Full 512-bit intermediate precision applies (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECORMath.sol).

## Fees

Fee values are the parameter sheet values of spec/release-v6-12-0/contracts/perps-parameters.md#spot-fees (heading "Spot fees"). The rules:

- Taker fee: swapFeeBps on the market and swap rail at 0x1021.
- Maker fee: orderFeeBps on resting fills at 0x1022 and 0x1023. orderFeeBps is the spec name, served by the application selector `orderFeeBps()`, of the order-engine base fee that the source names swapFeeBps (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol).
- Order pricing set: the order rails keep their own tiers and impact settings, as the source keeps one pricing set per engine (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol): orderFeeBps, orderTier1FeeBps, orderTier2FeeBps, orderPriceImpactEnabled and orderPriceImpactScalarBps, whose defaults equal the swap-rail values (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol) except orderFeeBps, which takes the parameter sheet value.
- Tiers: the tier-1 fee is added from 10,000 USD volume and the tier-2 fee on top from 100,000 USD (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol).
- Impact: the impact deduction is capped at MAX_IMPACT_BPS 500 (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol).
- Accrual: the retained fee and impact deduction stay in custody as an accrual of the output token, reserved so that no liquidity provider can withdraw them (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol), and grow `accumulatedFees(address)` of the rail that traded. The swap-rail accrual is separate from the order-rail accrual, which 0x1022 and 0x1023 share. Fees and impact belong to feeCollector, not to the liquidity providers.
- Fee events: TieredFeeApplied and PriceImpactApplied are emitted at 0x1021 only, on the exact-input paths that run the fee step (swapExactIn, marketBuy, marketSell, swapExactInNative and swapExactInToNative; contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol). swapExactOut (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol) and the order fills (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol) emit neither, as the source, and the order ABIs carry neither.
- Collection: feeCollector collects an accrual with `collectFees(address)`; collection pays the accrual and releases its reservation (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol).
- Validation: Params.Validate refuses a swap fee stack above MAX_FEE_BPS 200 (`FeeTooHigh()`) and a tier-2 fee below the tier-1 fee (`Tier2BelowTier1()`) (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol); a priceImpactScalarBps above MAX_IMPACT_BPS 500 (`ScalarTooHigh()`, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol); and for the order pricing set the bounds of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol: orderTier2FeeBps below orderTier1FeeBps (`Tier2BelowTier1()`), any order fee above 200 or the order stack above MAX_ORDER_FEE_BPS 200 (`FeeExceedsMax()`), and orderPriceImpactScalarBps above 500 (`ScalarTooHigh()`).

## TWAP

- Sampling: each block the keeper records (block time, index price) per non-stable token into a ring of 1000 samples, the depth of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/oracle/PriceOracle.sol.
- Read: `getTWAP(address,uint256)` returns the time-weighted index over the period (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/oracle/PriceOracle.sol). A zero period or one longer than the ring covers reverts `TwapWindowInvalid()`; a period above twapMaxPeriod in a parameter change is refused with `InvalidTWAPPeriod()`.
- Sanity: with twapSanityEnabled, a trade whose index deviates from its TWAP over twapSanityPeriod by more than maxTwapDeviationBps reverts `StalePrice()`. The default is off.
- Fill quality: fillQualityTwapPeriod, when non-zero, holds an EndBlock fill whose index deviates from the TWAP of that period by more than maxTwapDeviationBps until a later block.
- Example: index 0.5e18 for 200 s then 0.6e18 for 100 s gives getTWAP over 300 s of 0.533333333333333333e18.

## Market and swap rail

0x1021 serves market orders, exact-in and exact-out swaps, native swaps and quotes (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol):

- Market orders: `marketBuy(address,address,uint256,uint256,uint256)` pulls the stable amount and pays the base token; `marketSell(address,address,uint256,uint256,uint256)` pulls the base and pays the stable token. One side must be the stable token (`NotAStablecoin()`, `TokenIsStablecoin()`). Both emit MarketOrderExecuted.
- Swaps: `swapExactIn` and `swapExactOut` take any two registered tokens and emit SimpleSwap; the same token on both sides reverts `SameToken()`. An exact-out input above maxAmountIn reverts `ExcessiveInput()`.
- Common checks: a deadline in the past reverts `Expired()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol); a zero amount reverts `ZeroAmount()`; an output below the minimum reverts `InsufficientOutput()`; a token whose paused flag is set reverts `TokenPaused()`.
- Liquidity check on gross: a trade whose gross output exceeds the spendable book of the output token, or its utilization cap, reverts `InsufficientLiquidity()`. The source reserves the retained fee and impact before it checks the net output (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol, then contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol and contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol), so the rule is gross at most spendable, which the order engine checks directly (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol).
- multicall: `multicall(bytes[])` is served nonpayable, as the source (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol), and runs each element in-process with the same caller, reverting with the first element error; an element that fails without data reverts `MulticallFailed(uint256)` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol). The application ABI declares it nonpayable (apps/perps/src/core/network/spot_abi/PECOR.ts).
- Plain value: a plain value transfer to 0x1021 reverts `NativeTransferFailed()`.

## Order lifecycle

Limit orders live on 0x1022; stop-limit and stop-loss orders live on 0x1023. Each kind has its own record and its own active index, and the three share one id counter starting at 1 (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol). The source keeps stop-loss orders in the limit record and list (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol); the application ABI gives them their own views at 0x1023 (apps/perps/src/core/network/spot_abi/PECORStopOrders.ts, apps/perps/src/core/network/spot_abi/PECORStopOrders.ts), so the 0x1022 lists never contain a STOP_LOSS id and `executeLimitOrder` never fills one. Escrow moves into custody at placement (ERC20 by transferFrom to the module EVM address, native by bank send) and is kept in the escrow record, disjoint from the book, so that no liquidity provider can withdraw it.

| Kind | Placed by | Escrow | Executes when | Fills at | Fill event |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LIMIT_BUY | `placeLimitBuy` | stable amount | index <= targetPrice | index price | LimitOrderExecuted at 0x1022 |
| LIMIT_SELL | `placeLimitSell` | base amount | index >= targetPrice | index price | LimitOrderExecuted at 0x1022 |
| STOP_LIMIT_BUY | `placeStopLimitBuy` | stable amount | activates at index >= stop, fills while index <= limit | index price | StopLimitExecuted at 0x1023 |
| STOP_LIMIT_SELL | `placeStopLimitSell` | base amount | activates at index <= stop, fills while index >= limit | index price | StopLimitExecuted at 0x1023 |
| STOP_LOSS | `placeStopLoss` | base amount | index <= triggerPrice | index price | StopLossOrderExecuted at 0x1023 |

Status values: PENDING 0, ACTIVATED 1, EXECUTED 2, CANCELLED 3, EXPIRED 4 (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/interfaces/IPECOROrders.sol).

- Placement: validation (registered tokens, one stable side, non-zero amount, price range, expiry in the future or 0 for none, token not paused, minimum notional, per-user cap) runs before any token move, so a refused order moves nothing (modules/spot/keeper/orders.go).
- Minimum notional: an order whose escrow value at the index price (amount x price of the input token / 10^decimals) is below minOrderNotional reverts `OrderBelowMinimumNotional()`.
- Per-user cap: a user who already has maxActiveOrdersPerUser active orders across the three kinds reverts `TooManyActiveOrders()`.
- Price range: a zero stopPrice or limitPrice reverts `InvalidPrices()`, the ABI name of the source `InvalidPrice()` of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol; a stop-limit buy with limit below stop reverts `LimitMustBeAboveStopBuy()` and a stop-limit sell with limit above stop reverts `LimitMustBeBelowStopSell()`, the ABI names of the source `InvalidPriceRange()` of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol.
- Minimum output: each order keeps orderMinOutput (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol), 0 for every placement through the application selectors, which take no minimum (as the source placement entry points pass 0, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol); a fill whose net output is below it, or zero, reverts `InsufficientOutput()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol).
- Cancel: the user of a PENDING order (or ACTIVATED stop-limit) cancels and the full escrow returns; any other caller reverts `NotOrderOwner()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol). A final limit or stop-limit reverts `CannotCancel()`, the ABI name of the source `OrderCannotCancel()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol) and, for limits, of the source `OrderNotPending()` (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol); a final stop-loss reverts `NotPending()`. Cancel works while ordersPaused.
- Modify: `modifyLimitOrder(uint256,uint256,uint256)` changes targetPrice and expiresAt of a PENDING limit order and leaves the escrow unchanged.
- Expiry: anyone may expire an order whose non-zero expiresAt is at or before the block time (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol); the order becomes EXPIRED and its escrow returns to its user, through `batchExpireOrders(uint256[])` for limit orders or the EndBlock sweep for every kind. A limit expiry emits LimitOrderExpired at 0x1022; a stop-limit expiry emits StopLimitCancelled and a stop-loss expiry StopLossOrderCancelled at 0x1023, the record status EXPIRED telling an expiry from a cancel.
- Fill: a fill pays the net output to the user (ERC20 transfer or bank send by the kind of the output token), releases the escrow and removes the id from its active index by swap and pop (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOROrders.sol).
- Fixture: placeLimitBuy(USDL, PAX, 100e6, 0.5e18, 0) as the first order returns id 1.

## EndBlock execution

The spot EndBlocker executes resting orders against the pool index price in every block, in the shape of the oracle EndBlocker (modules/oracle/abci.go). The node calls it from the legacy end block (node/legacyabci/end_block.go) in the order oracle, perps, spot, EVM; the pricefeed aggregates in MidBlock (SetOrderMidBlockers(oracle, pricefeed)), so the index read here is the one of the current block. Every step has a budget and a persisted cursor, so the work of one block is bounded by the sum of the budgets whatever the number of active orders:

1. Record one TWAP sample per non-stable token (bounded by the registered token count).
2. Expire orders past expiresAt from the expiry index ordered by (expiresAt, id), at most maxExpiriesPerBlock.
3. Activation scan: examine at most activationScanPerBlock PENDING stop-limits of the active stop-limit index from the activation cursor and activate those whose stop condition holds (StopLimitActivated).
4. Fill scan: examine at most fillScanPerBlock entries of the activated stop-limits, then the limit index, then the stop-loss index, from the fill cursor, and fill those whose condition holds, at most maxFillsPerBlock fills.

Rules for every step:

- Cursors: scans resume at persisted cursors and wrap at the end of their indexes. Scan, attempt and fill budgets bound each block; tombstones, fill limits and back-off can delay an order, so there is no fixed examination deadline.
- Back-off: a fill that fails (stale price, insufficient liquidity, fill-quality hold, output below the order minimum) leaves the order unchanged, increments its failure count and sets its next-retry height to the current height + min(2^failures, maxFillBackoffBlocks); scans skip an order until that height. A fill, cancel or expiry deletes the back-off record.
- Placement bounds: minOrderNotional and maxActiveOrdersPerUser bound the growth of the indexes at placement (Order lifecycle).
- Isolation: each fill runs in its own cached context; a failed fill writes nothing but its back-off record.
- Payouts: ERC20 payouts run through CallEVM from the module address, native payouts by bank send (Token model). No EndBlock step mints or burns shares.
- Logs: the EndBlocker runs under SyntheticReceiptContext(ctx, SyntheticReceiptSpot), the receipt whose hash is keccak256("spot-endblock" || height as 8 big-endian bytes). The USDL Transfer logs of CallEVM merge into that receipt (modules/evm/keeper/evm.go), and the spot logs are appended to the same receipt at its hash and provisional index (GetTransientReceipt and SetTransientReceipt, modules/evm/keeper/receipt.go, each log converted by ConvertSyntheticEthLog, modules/evm/keeper/log.go) with its bloom recomputed. The receipt flusher sets its TransactionIndex to the highest real index + 2 (perps + 1, activation + 3) and serves it through eth_getLogs, pax_getLogs, eth_getBlockReceipts, eth_getTransactionReceipt and the block bloom.
- Same rule in transactions: `executeLimitOrder`, `executeStopLimitOrder`, `executeStopLossOrder` and the batch methods apply the same rule inside a transaction when keeperOpen is true or the caller is a listed keeper; otherwise they revert `NotAuthorized()`. Their logs are the logs of that transaction.

## Vault share accounting

0x1025 holds one pool per token. Liquidity providers deposit a token and receive shares of that token's pool. Shares are bank coins of the share denom with an ERC20 native pointer served by NativePaxTokensERC20 (contracts/src/NativePaxTokensERC20.sol:7, contracts/src/NativePaxTokensERC20.sol:33-43). The v2 vault issues no shares (Implementation), so the rules below are defined by this contract.

```
custody(T)    = balanceOf(module EVM address) for an ERC20 token, bank balance of the module account for a native token
escrow(T)     = sum of the escrow of active orders whose input is T            disjoint from book(T)
reserved(T)   = swap-rail accrual(T) + order-rail accrual(T)                   retained fees and impact
spendable(T)  = max(0, min(custody(T) - escrow(T), book(T)) - reserved(T))
NAV(T)        = spendable(T)
supply(T)     = bank supply of the share denom of T, MINIMUM_SHARES included
first deposit : amount <= MINIMUM_SHARES reverts ZeroAmount(); else mints amount - MINIMUM_SHARES to the depositor and MINIMUM_SHARES to the module account
later deposit : NAV == 0 reverts InsufficientLiquidity(); shares = amount * supply / NAV rounded down; 0 shares reverts ZeroAmount()
withdraw      : amount paid = shares * NAV / supply                             rounded down
withdrawNative: shares burned = amount * supply / NAV                           rounded up
```

- MINIMUM_SHARES is 1000. These are the initial shares burned on the first deposit of each token: the module account holds them, counts them in supply and never redeems them, so the first depositor cannot set the share price by a dust deposit.
- Book: a deposit adds its measured receipt to book(T); a withdraw subtracts the payout. A trade adds the input to the book of its token and subtracts the net output from the book of the output token, the retained part staying in the book as a reservation (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol, contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol); both books emit ReservesUpdated. Escrow enters the book only when a fill consumes it.
- Value between pools: a trade moves value between the pools of its two tokens at the index price, as the source does (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/engine/PECOR.sol): the input pool gains amountIn and the output pool gives gross, of equal USD value at the index within the rounding of gross. The bounds on how much one block can take from a pool are the utilization cap per trade, the per-token paused flag and the TWAP sanity rule; the conservation invariant (Invariants) checks the equality on every trade.
- Utilization cap: maxUtilizationBps of each token is defined by this contract (the source check of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol has no cap): a trade or fill whose gross output exceeds spendable(T) x maxUtilizationBps / 10000 of its output token reverts `InsufficientLiquidity()`. DEFAULT_MAX_UTILIZATION_BPS 10000 applies to a token without its own cap; the opening tokens take 9000.
- hasLiquidity(T, amount) is true when T is registered and not paused, amount <= spendable(T) and amount x 10000 <= spendable(T) x maxUtilizationBps(T).
- NAV zero: with supply above zero and NAV 0, deposit and depositNative revert `InsufficientLiquidity()`; a withdraw whose payout exceeds spendable reverts `InsufficientLiquidity()`.
- Reconciliation: syncReserves and syncAllReserves are governance-only, as the source restricts them to DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE (contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol): an EVM caller reverts `OwnableUnauthorizedAccount(address)`, and MsgSyncReserves signed by the governance authority runs the source reconciliation of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/vault/PECORVault.sol over custody(T) - escrow(T), emitting ReservesSync. Until governance reconciles, a donation sent straight to custody raises no NAV, because spendable takes the minimum of custody and book.
- Worked example: an empty USDL pool takes 100,000e6; the depositor receives 99,999,999,000 shares (100,000e6 - 1000) and the module account 1000, supply 100,000e6. At supply 100,000e6 and NAV 110,000e6, a deposit of 11,000e6 mints 10,000e6 shares. At supply 110,000e6 and NAV 121,000e6, withdrawing 10,000e6 shares pays 11,000e6.
- NAV example: book 100,000e6, escrow 1,000e6 (custody 101,000e6), accrual 500e6 gives NAV 99,500e6.
- Shares are bank coins: shares transfer freely through the pointer and the holder withdraws with them. Share mint and burn happen only in transactions (deposit, depositBatch, depositNative, withdraw, withdrawNative), and each emits the share Transfer log at the share pointer (Events).

## Revert encoding

A failed call returns the 4-byte selector of its ABI error followed by its encoded arguments. It does not use Error(string): the spot Run reuses Prepare (precompiles/common/precompiles.go) and writes the custom error data before vm.ErrExecutionReverted (precompiles/common/precompiles.go). An internal error that has no ABI error packs as Error(string) through RevertReason (precompiles/common/precompiles.go). The errors an address returns are the error entries of its application ABI plus the entries of Added error entries; the served JSON of precompiles/spot carries both, and clients decode these errors using the served JSON interface. Params.Validate errors are module errors of MsgUpdateParams and never reach an EVM caller. Example: marketBuy reverting on slippage returns exactly 0xbb2875c3. A reverted call writes no log.

## PECORQuoter interface

PECORQuoter is served at 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000001024. Every row keeps the selector and full signature of the application ABI.

QuoteResult has 23 fields. The first 14 are the fields of contracts/launchpad/contracts/meta-ag/interfaces/IMetaAGQuoter.sol, with availableLiquidity the spendable book of the output token. The other nine are defined by this contract:

- utilizationCapBps: the maxUtilizationBps of the output token, 10000 when unset.
- tradeUtilizationBps: gross x 10000 / spendable of the output token, rounded up; the maximum uint256 when spendable is 0.
- cappedByUtilization: tradeUtilizationBps above utilizationCapBps.
- twapOut and twapDeviationBps: the TWAP of the output token over twapSanityPeriod and the deviation of its index from it in bps, 0 for a stable token.
- effectiveFeeBps: equal to feeBps, the base fee plus the tiers that apply.
- tier: 0, 1 or 2, the highest volume tier reached.
- priceImpactBps and priceImpactDeduction: the impact in bps and the amount it deducts from the output.
- sufficientLiquidity: true when cappedByUtilization is false and gross is at most spendable.

## Events

One row per event with its full signature. topic0 is keccak256 of the canonical signature; indexed fields follow as topics in ABI order and the rest is ABI-encoded data. Logs are written through EmitEVMLog (precompiles/common/evm_events.go) at the address in the third column. The order creation events carry no token address, as the application ABI fixes them; a reader resolves the tokens of an order id by eth_call of getLimitOrder at 0x1022, getStopLimitOrder or getStopLossOrder at 0x1023. The venue keeps no order book: resting orders fill at the index price, so no book view or book event exists. Each share pointer is named by the ShareTokenCreated log of its registration, so a reader that follows 0x1025 learns every pointer address before its first share Transfer.

## Storage

The spot module owns one KV store under StoreKey "spot" with one-byte prefixes, in the shape of modules/launchpad/types/keys.go:

| Prefix | Key | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 0x01 | params | Params (governance parameters and token list with kind, decimals, market id, paused flag and cap) |
| 0x02 | token address | book reserves, total deposited, total withdrawn, decimals, share denom, MINIMUM_SHARES locked flag |
| 0x03 | token address | escrow reserved by active orders, disjoint from the book |
| 0x04 | rail byte, token address | fee accrual (swap rail or order rail) |
| 0x05 | none | next order id, starting at 1, shared by the three kinds |
| 0x06 | order id | LimitOrder record (limit buy and limit sell) |
| 0x07 | order id | StopLimitOrder record |
| 0x08 | user address, order id | index of orders per user, with the kind |
| 0x09 | order id | active limit index |
| 0x0A | order id | active stop-limit index |
| 0x0B | order id | active stop-loss index |
| 0x0C | token address, slot | TWAP sample (time, index) |
| 0x0D | token address | TWAP ring head and count |
| 0x0E | token address | traded volume and trade count |
| 0x0F | order id | StopLossOrder record |
| 0x10 | order id | orderMinOutput of the order |
| 0x11 | expiresAt, order id | expiry index of every active order with a non-zero expiresAt |
| 0x12 | cursor byte | EndBlock cursors (activation scan, fill scan) |
| 0x13 | order id | fill back-off record (failure count, next-retry height) |
| 0x14 | user address | active order count across the three kinds |

GenesisState carries every prefix and round-trips through InitGenesis and ExportGenesis (modules/spot/keeper/genesis.go); genesis validation refuses an active index entry without its record, an escrow that differs from the sum of its active orders and an active count that differs from the user's active orders; the share-backing invariant checks the share supply against MINIMUM_SHARES (Invariants).

## Activation

modules/spot/keeper/init_v6120.go func InitV6120(ctx sdk.Context, k Keeper) error is the spot hook of the v6.12.0 upgrade handler. The hook table runs, in order, the pointer step createV6120Pointers (node/upgrades_v6120_pointers.go, which writes the uhpx metadata, checks the SID denom metadata and creates both native pointers through UpsertERCNativePointer), launchpad MigrateV6120, pricefeed InitV6120, perps SeedV6120 and spot InitV6120, then sets the v6120Live flag. Spot InitV6120:

1. Refuses a second run with "spot: already initialised" when the params key is set.
2. Registers, in this order, USDL as stable with kind ERC20 at contracts/libraries/Constants.sol:14, reading decimals() through StaticCallEVM and requiring the pinned 6 (contracts/libraries/Constants.sol:16); uhpx with kind NATIVE and market 11 at its native pointer; and the SID denom layerxbridgetypes.SidioraDenom() with kind NATIVE and market 12 at its native pointer, reading each native decimals from the denom metadata exponent and requiring the pinned 6. A pointer that is absent, metadata that is absent or decimals other than 6 return an error that aborts the plan before any spot state is written. USDL has no pointer and no bank denom.
3. Creates the share denoms spot/share/<token>, their metadata and pointers through UpsertERCNativePointer (modules/evm/keeper/pointer_upgrade.go) inside RunWithOneOffEVMInstance (modules/evm/keeper/pointer_upgrade.go); the spot module account holds Minter and Burner, and the keeper refuses to mint or burn any denom outside spot/share/*.
4. Writes the default parameters.
5. Writes the activation logs into the activation synthetic receipt (SyntheticReceiptActivation, TransactionIndex the highest real index + 3): OwnershipTransferred(address(0), governance) at 0x1021, 0x1022, 0x1023 and 0x1025 only; OperatorUpdated for the three rails; TokenRegistered, ShareTokenCreated and UtilizationCapUpdated per token; TransactionTrackerUpdated(address(0)).

## Invariants

- Solvency: for each token, custody (balanceOf of the module EVM address for an ERC20 token, the module bank balance for a native token) is at least the book reserves plus the escrow, and the book reserves are at least the fee accruals.
- Escrow: escrow is disjoint from the book; every active index holds only orders of its kind in PENDING status (or ACTIVATED for stop-limits), and the escrow of a token equals the sum of amounts of its active orders.
- Share backing: the bank supply of a share denom is zero exactly when its pool never took a deposit, and is at least MINIMUM_SHARES after the first; NAV is the spendable reserves.
- Conservation: every trade and fill adds amountIn to the book of its input token and removes the net output from the book of its output token, and the USD value at the index of amountIn equals the USD value of gross within the rounding of gross; checked by TestCrossPoolConservation.
- Bounded EndBlock: every EndBlock step examines at most its budget of orders, whatever the length of the indexes.
- Atomicity: every call and every EndBlock fill writes pull, payout, book and accrual together or not at all.
- ABI: the rail interfaces are defined in the rail JSON files under `precompiles/spot/abi`; the deployed share-pointer interface is `precompiles/spot/abi/share.json`.

The spot-solvency, share-backing, escrow and conservation invariants are registered as launchpad registers its own (modules/launchpad/keeper/invariants.go).

## Hyperpax call sequence

The spot fixture used by every normative example:

- Prices: USDL 1e18, PAX index 0.5e18 (market 11), SID index 2e18 (market 12), all fresh.
- Books: 500,000e6 USDL, 1,000,000e6 PAX and 250,000e6 SID deposited by one provider, no escrow, no accrual.
- Parameters: swapFeeBps 30, tier1FeeBps 20, tier2FeeBps 50, priceImpactEnabled true with scalar 100, orderFeeBps 10 with the order tiers and impact at the swap-rail values, keeperOpen true.
- Users: one user holding 1,000,000e6 USDL as an ERC20 balance and 1,000,000e6 uhpx.
